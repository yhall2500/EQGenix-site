EQGENIX AI ASSURANCE

Build the Human. Govern the Technology.™

Human Capability Assurance for the Age of AI.

Artificial intelligence is becoming part of professional work across institutions. But technology alone cannot establish whether the person responsible for an outcome possesses the judgment, competence, and accountability required to stand behind it.

EQGenix AI Assurance helps institutions examine what people can demonstrate independently, what they accomplish with AI assistance, what they can verify, and what capability remains when the technology is withdrawn.

THE PRINCIPLE

Technology Governance Begins With Human Authority.

Every institution will deploy AI. Within a few years, the question leaders face will shift from whether to use it to who can be trusted to govern it. Policies and tools matter, and they depend on a capable human at the point of decision. EQGenix assures that human.

“Technology governance begins with the person who approves, signs, and answers for the output.”

The aim is not simply AI literacy, software adoption, or training completion. It is observable evidence of human capability.

THE ASSURANCE FRAMEWORK

Authority · Verification · Evidence

AUTHORITY
Who decides?

Each AI-assisted workflow is mapped to the human role that owns the decision, the record, and the outcome.

VERIFICATION
What gets checked?

People are tested on finding errors, gaps, and unsupported claims in AI work before they sign.

EVIDENCE
What can they show?

Independent performance is observed after the tool is withdrawn, on a new task, and after time has passed.

HOW THE ENGAGEMENT WORKS

Assess · Form · Re-assure · Report

01

ASSESS

Baseline independent capability against the duties of the role.

02

FORM

Formation AI™ builds capability against the findings through six phases.

DEMONSTRATE→AUGMENT→INTERROGATE→RECONSTRUCT→WITHDRAW→TRANSFER
03

RE-ASSURE

Withdrawal, transfer, and delayed-retention checks confirm the capability holds.

04

REPORT

Leaders receive capability by role and cohort. Individual results stay protected.

This is the documented assurance model. Stages are applied according to each engagement and program; not every stage or reporting feature is in place for every program.
