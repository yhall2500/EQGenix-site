Assessment Use Policy

This policy governs every capacity measurement EQGenix administers, in every program. It appears identically here and in the master services agreement, because an undisclosed protection cannot be relied on by a buyer — or by a participant.

NON-WAIVABLE RULES
1

No individual profile is released to any employer, agency, school, court, or funder.

2

No capacity score informs any personnel, disciplinary, academic, legal, or fitness-for-duty decision.

3

Employer and institutional reporting is aggregate only, with a stated minimum cell size below which no segment is reported.

4

The participant owns their record — The Holdings™, the adult lifetime capacity record — and may delete it on request. It is portable across employers.

5

These rules appear identically on this website and in the master services agreement, and no engagement term may waive them.

DATA PRACTICES

What is collected: instrument responses and the capacity profile derived from them, held in the participant's record — The Holdings™ for adults, participant-owned, portable across employers, and deletable on request. Retention runs until the participant requests deletion; the deletion path is a written request to contact@eqgenix.com, honored without condition. Individual data is visible to the participant and to the EQGenix measurement function — no one else. No participant data is sold, licensed, or used for behavioral ad tracking, ever.

EMPLOYMENT-DECISION USE — CALIFORNIA POSTURE

These instruments are designed and contracted so that no output can lawfully or practically function as an employment screen. California's Fair Employment and Housing Act reaches further than the federal ADA, and this policy is written against that broader standard: no capacity score is delivered in a form usable in any personnel, disciplinary, or fitness-for-duty decision, and the master services agreement prohibits the attempt.

PUBLIC-AGENCY ENGAGEMENTS & PUBLIC RECORDS

Reports delivered to a public agency may be subject to disclosure under the California Public Records Act. The aggregate-only architecture is the deliberate design response to that exposure: a disclosed report contains no individual's data, because no individual's data is ever in it.

WHAT THESE INSTRUMENTS ARE

EQGenix instruments measure capacity — the trained, observable ability to perform under demand. They are not clinical instruments. They do not diagnose, screen for, or evaluate any medical or psychological condition, and no output should be read as doing so. No instrument in this system assigns a deficit label or classification to a person.

SCOPE

This policy covers every EQGenix measurement instrument across all programs and engagements, including individual entry and exit measurement and organization-level aggregate audits. Where a specific engagement adds protections, they add to this floor; nothing subtracts from it. Questions about this policy: contact@eqgenix.com.
