Certified Grit to Genius Facilitator™ (Founding Cohort)

Part-time, per-cohort·Canyon Lake / Riverside County, in person
THE WORK You will lead cohorts of twelve adolescents through a six-week identity formation arc. Not tutoring, not therapy, not supervision — you are running a room where young people build a personal code they can actually use under pressure, and then declare it in front of their families.
THE CERTIFICATION Facilitation requires completion of the Certified Grit to Genius Facilitator™ (CGGF™) Tier I program — a structured curriculum plus a supervised practicum. Certification is the credential; the founding cohort of facilitators is being built now.
WHO THIS FITS People with real experience holding adolescents — teachers, coaches, youth pastors, counselors, group leaders. What matters is whether you can keep twelve teenagers honest for ninety minutes and not flinch when one of them tells the truth.
REQUIREMENTS Background screening and Live Scan. Mandated reporter status. Reliable transportation. Availability for a full six-week cohort arc without gaps.
WHAT THIS IS NOT You will not diagnose, treat, or counsel. Facilitators work inside a defined scope with documented referral protocols, and we hold that line hard.

REQUIREMENTS Background screening. Comfort with participant records and the discretion that requires. Fluent with modern tools; strong writing is not optional.
