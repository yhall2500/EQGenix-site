THE LEDGER BENEATH EVERY EQGENIX PROGRAM

Genius Capacity Index™

One person, one record, one curve. Every EQGenix instrument writes to a single lifetime capacity record — so a reading taken at twelve and a reading taken at twenty-six sit on the same curve, not in unrelated files.

The Premise

A Delta Is a Result.
A Record Is
Infrastructure.

Grit to Genius™ measures a young person twice — once before the first session and once at the sixth. That produces a delta. The Genius Capacity Index™ and The Account™ make it infrastructure: a single, person-scoped, lifelong capacity record that every EQGenix instrument writes into.

A person who moves through Community at thirteen, Level Two at fourteen, and Mission-Ready Civilian™ at twenty-six holds one curve across thirteen years — possible only because every EQGenix program measures against the same construct spine: the Emotional Portfolio™.

THE DEFECT IT FIXES Cohort-scoped IDs die when the cohort ends. Under that scheme, a participant who returns four months later is a new person to the system — and the six-week delta is orphaned the day it becomes most valuable.
THE DECISION Longitudinal capacity is not a reporting feature added later. It is an identifier decision made at first contact — a cohort ID becomes an attribute of an enrollment record, never an identity.
THE ARCHITECTURE

Three Layers, One Ledger.

01
The EQGenix Capacity ID

A person-scoped identifier issued once, at first contact with any EQGenix program, and never reissued. It encodes nothing — not birth year, not program, not band.

LIFESPAN · LIFETIME, OR UNTIL THE HOLDER REQUESTS DESTRUCTION
02
The Enrollment Record

One per program the person passes through. Carries cohort ID, band, edition, dates, facilitator, and completion status.

LIFESPAN · PROGRAM DURATION + RETENTION PERIOD
03
The Measurement Event

One per instrument administration. Carries timepoint, instrument and version, the five pillar scores, CSS, The Gap™, and the modifiers declared.

LIFESPAN · RETAINED UNDER THE CONSENT THAT AUTHORISED IT
EQG-XXXXXX

Six characters from an unambiguous alphabet — I, O, 0, and 1 excluded — with a checksum on the final character. The name-to-ID key is a separate document, stored separately, never held in the same file as any response data.

CONSENT ARCHITECTURE

Four Signatures. Never Bundled.

A longitudinal record is a materially different thing from a program record. Each consent stands alone, and each is separately revocable.

REQUIRED
Program Participation

Attendance, delivery, safety, and emergency contact.

OPTIONAL
Program Measurement

Entry and Exit administration, scored for the participant's own Genius Profile™. A participant may decline and remain fully enrolled.

OPTIONAL
Research Use

De-identified aggregate use in reporting, grant, and publication contexts.

OPTIONAL · SEPARATELY REVOCABLE
Longitudinal Record

Retention of the ID and measurement events beyond this program, for use in later EQGenix programs. Separate signature.

Under-13 longitudinal consent requires verifiable parental consent under the amended COPPA Rule. Revocation is honoured within 30 days: the key is destroyed first, then the linked records — both logged.

At 18, the holder is contacted and re-consents in their own name. A record held on an adult under a consent their parent signed at twelve is not a record they consented to.

PORTABILITY

A school gives a child a transcript of what they were taught. Nobody gives a child a record of what they built.

The holder owns their record. On request, a participant or their guardian receives the full Account™ in a readable export — every measurement event, every pillar score, every Atlas shape, every deposit, with dates and instrument versions. Your child leaves owning their portfolio.
