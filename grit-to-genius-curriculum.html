GRIT TO GENIUS™ HOMESCHOOL THE CURRICULUM

The student does more than read about the principle.

The curriculum is built on deliberate work and reflection rather than passive content consumption. Each week the student reads a principle, does the work, records the evidence, and writes the debrief — with a note telling the parent exactly what to watch for.

View The Age Bands SEE ONE WEEK ↓
THE LEARNING MODEL

Principle in. Practice out.

Principle→ Work→ Effort / Decision→ Evidence→ Reflection→ Adult Observation→ Next Practice

The loop repeats weekly. Nothing advances on reading alone — the student moves forward on documented work.

ONE WEEK OF NAVIGATORS™

Six parts. Every week.

01

Lesson

The week's principle, written for the student to read independently.

02

Scripture Anchor

The passage the week rests on, with its function identified — Governing or Illuminating.

03

The Work

The student's central task for the week, designed to be completed independently.

04

Printable Set

Worksheets and pages where the student records the work.

05

Reflection

A written debrief completed after the work.

06

Parent Note

Guidance explaining what the adult should watch for, what not to rescue, and when to step in.

THE YEAR

Six units. Thirty-six weeks.

Each unit runs six weeks and closes with a checkpoint. The year ends with a capstone the student builds, presents, and defends.

UNIT 01WEEKS 1–6
Identity & Ownership

Self-knowledge, personal responsibility, and an internal standard.

UNIT 02WEEKS 7–12
Emotional Regulation

Recognize internal signals, tolerate discomfort, and choose a response.

UNIT 03WEEKS 13–18
Cognitive Sovereignty™

Independent thought, evidence discernment, and technology without outsourcing judgment.

UNIT 04WEEKS 19–24
Applied Problem-Solving

From answer-seeking to problem ownership: question, decide, test, revise.

UNIT 05WEEKS 25–30
Communication & Influence

Listen, disagree well, set boundaries, persuade ethically, repair conflict.

UNIT 06WEEKS 31–36
Character, Leadership & Commissioning

Demonstrated character, service, and an original capstone contribution.

Navigators™ (ages 11–13) and Sovereigns™ (ages 14–17) move through the same six-unit arc, with the work tuned to each stage.

THE SCRIPTURE ANCHOR

Two functions. Always named.

Every week identifies how its passage is working. The exercises, assignments, and assessments are designed to develop capability.

Governing

The passage is the authority the week's principle rests on.

Illuminating

The passage names the human reality the week's work exposes.
