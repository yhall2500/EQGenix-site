WHY HOMESCHOOL

Information is abundant.Formation is becoming scarce.

Young people have unprecedented access to answers. But information access does not automatically develop sustained attention, tolerance for struggle, independent judgment, discernment, reflection, responsibility, communication, or self-leadership.

Grit to Genius™ Homeschool creates structured opportunities to practice these capacities — at home, at the family's own pace.

INSIDE THE CURRICULUM

The student does more than read about the principle.

Every Navigators™ week moves through six parts — deliberate work and reflection, not passive content consumption.

01

Lesson

The week's principle, written for the student to read independently.

02

Scripture Anchor

The passage the week rests on, with its function identified.

03

The Work

The student's central task for the week, designed to be completed independently.

04

Printable Set

Worksheets and pages where the student records the work.

05

Reflection

A written debrief completed after the work.

06

Parent Note

Guidance explaining what the adult should watch for, what not to rescue, and when to step in.

Questions, answered.

What is Grit to Genius™ Homeschool?+

A self-paced youth formation curriculum developed within the Grit to Genius™ platform.

Who is Navigators™ for?+

Ages 11–13.

How long is Navigators™?+

36 weeks.

Is it live?+

No. Navigators™ is 100% self-paced — no live instruction, scheduled sessions, scheduled classes, tutoring, or instructor contact.

Is it Christian?+

Yes. Each week includes a Scripture Anchor, used in two identified functions: Governing — the passage is the authority the week's principle rests on — and Illuminating — the passage names the human reality the week's work exposes.

What does the family receive?+

A 12-month digital license to the 36-week Navigators™ curriculum with printable student materials, six unit checkpoints, a weekly progress log, student portfolio pages, written assignments, and a Certificate of Completion. Delivery is digital — nothing ships.

Is this the same as Grit to Genius™ Community?+

No. Community is the live facilitated experience. Homeschool is the self-paced curriculum.

Can schools use Grit to Genius™?+

Education organizations can explore the institutional implementation pathway through Grit to Genius™ Schools & Education Partners.

Where can I review the full curriculum?+

The complete curriculum review, product details, policies, and purchase live on the dedicated site: grittogenius.com/curriculum.
