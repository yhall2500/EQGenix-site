Measured Outcomes

The Numbers Arrive With the Evidence.

Every engagement runs entry and exit measurement — the GCI in youth cohorts, the Emotional Wealth Diagnostic elsewhere. As implementation data is validated, aggregate outcomes are published here with methodology notes, not as marketing claims.

I
WHAT IS MEASURED

Entry-to-exit change across the five pillars, per cohort and per engagement.

II
WHEN IT PUBLISHES

After validation and partner approval — aspirations are never published as accomplishments.

III
HOW IT IS VERIFIED

Documented instruments, defined baselines, and methodology notes accompany every published figure.

IV
WHAT PARTNERS RECEIVE

Aggregate development reporting for institutions; individual results stay with the individual.
