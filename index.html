02 / What we mean by formation

Knowing is
a beginning.
Becoming takes
practice.

Explore our approach →

Formation is the deliberate practice of how you think, respond, relate, and follow through.

Our programs put human capacity into everyday work: examining a decision, practicing a response, reflecting on what happened, and trying again with intention.

01

Think with independence.

Examine information. Explain your reasoning. Own the decision.

02

Respond with intention.

Notice pressure. Practice regulation. Choose how to act.

03

Carry it into real life.

Apply the practice in relationships, responsibilities, and work.

These are formation aims, not guaranteed program outcomes.
