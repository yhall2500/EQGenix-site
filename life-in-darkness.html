THE INSTRUMENT

Five things. Measured on a person, not a population.

The Emotional Portfolio™ measures five capacities. Not personality. Not diagnosis. Not whether someone is broken. Capacity — the amount of load a person can carry before it costs them something, and how fast they come back.

Cognitive Sovereignty™ Whether your thinking is still yours under pressure. Who is driving when it gets loud?
Emotional Regulation™ What it costs you to stay level, and how fast you recover. What is the bill after the call?
Resilience Architecture™ What you are built on when the thing you were built on gives out. What holds when the floor does not?
Relational Intelligence™ Whether you can still be reached by the people who love you. Can anyone get to you?
Effort Tolerance™ How long you can do hard things that do not pay off yet. How long can you keep going without applause?

Five scores. One person. Taken at entry, taken again over a career, and never averaged into a crowd.

The Emotional Portfolio is a formation instrument. It is not a clinical diagnostic, not a psychological evaluation, not a fitness-for-duty determination, and it is not used in hiring, promotion, discipline, or assignment. Individual results belong to the individual. What a department receives is aggregate movement, never a name.
