Canyon Lake Police and Fire Partner with EQGenix to Invest in Those Who Protect and Serve

Sovereign Shield™ and Sovereign Station™ focus on sustained readiness and performance under the demands of public safety service.

CANYON LAKE, Calif. — Nov. 10, 2026 — EQGenix Inc., a Canyon Lake–based human formation company, today announced partnerships with Canyon Lake Police Department and Canyon Lake Fire Department through Sovereign Shield™ and Sovereign Station™, programs designed to support the people who serve as the community’s first line of defense.

Sovereign Shield™ serves law enforcement personnel, while Sovereign Station™ serves fire personnel. Both programs focus on developing and sustaining the human capacity that demanding public safety roles require—on duty, across a career, and beyond the uniform.

POST STATUS Sovereign Shield™ Tier I has received California POST course certification approval.

The partnerships reflect a shared commitment to investing in the people behind public safety. Participation and scheduling details will be coordinated with each department.

This is the first step. More will be shared in the coming months about how this work will reach Canyon Lake families and the broader community.

“Every day, our police officers navigate challenging and often demanding situations. Ensuring they have the tools and resources to remain mentally and emotionally resilient is of great importance to officer wellness. Our collaboration with EQGenix reflects our unwavering commitment to supporting those who serve and protect our community.”

JIM RAYLS · CANYON LAKE POLICE CHIEF

“Our firefighters face demanding situations every day, and it is important that we provide them with the tools and resources to remain physically, mentally, and emotionally prepared throughout their careers. Our partnership with EQGenix is another step in our commitment to taking care of those who dedicate their lives to taking care of our community.”

JEFF LATENDRESSE · CANYON LAKE FIRE CHIEF

“Our police officers and firefighters are our community’s first line of defense. We invest in their equipment, their training, and their technology. We must also invest in the people carrying that responsibility.”

YVONNE G. HALL · FOUNDER & CEO, EQGENIX
ABOUT EQGENIX INC.

EQGenix Inc. is a Canyon Lake–based human formation company. It builds lasting human capacity, including sound judgment, resilience, and readiness, in the people communities depend on most: police officers, firefighters, workers, families, and youth. Through its founding partnerships with Canyon Lake Police Department and Canyon Lake Fire Department, EQGenix is bringing structured, practiced formation into the Canyon Lake community, serving residents across Canyon Lake and neighboring communities.
