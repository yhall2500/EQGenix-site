EQGenix Founder & CEO Yvonne G. Hall Releases Sacred Deposits™, a Blueprint for Protecting Covenant Marriage

EQGenix proudly celebrates the release of Sacred Deposits™: The Blueprint for Protecting Your Marriage, written by Founder and Chief Executive Officer Yvonne G. Hall and published on May 25, 2026.

Rooted in behavioral research, biblical principles, and nearly three decades of marriage experience, Sacred Deposits™ introduces a practical framework designed to help couples intentionally build, protect, and strengthen their covenant relationships.

At the heart of the book is the 5:1 principle, emphasizing the importance of consistent positive interactions, intentional communication, accountability, and relational repair.

Rather than viewing marriage as something sustained by emotion alone, Hall challenges couples to recognize that lasting relationships require deliberate investments.

The publication represents an important milestone in EQGenix’s broader mission to Build Emotional Wealth™. Through its Sacred Deposits™ division, EQGenix extends its emotional and cognitive development methodology into marriage and family formation.

Available in hardcover, paperback, and digital formats, the book invites couples to approach their relationships with renewed purpose, practical tools, and a commitment to building a legacy that extends beyond their generation.

A BLUEPRINT, NOT A DEVOTIONAL

Most marriages don’t fail. They erode.

The distance in a marriage rarely arrives all at once. It grows through patterns repeated so often they become invisible. Sacred Deposits™ names those patterns and gives a couple the structure to interrupt them before they become the story.

The book translates the 5:1™ framework, five deposits for every withdrawal, into a daily operating model grounded in behavioral science and covenant commitment. What a couple walks away with is language, structure, and a discipline they can keep.

Most marriage resources work in crisis. This one works upstream, for the couple that is still intact, still functioning, and privately aware that something needs strengthening before it breaks.

“Most marriages don’t end in betrayal. They erode in the unnamed.”

YVONNE G. HALL · FOUNDER & CEO, EQGENIX
WHERE THE BOOK COMES FROM

Built inside a covenant, sharpened in the courtroom.

Hall is a NAMA CAMS-II certified Applied Behavioral Researcher and a former Guardian ad Litem in the Florida and California courts, the setting where families often arrive when the account is already empty. She carried home the conviction the book is built on: emotional intelligence is the infrastructure a marriage stands or falls on.

She is also the mother of seven and grandmother of twelve, and she has been married for nearly three decades. The frameworks in Sacred Deposits™ were developed inside that marriage, which she describes as the laboratory for everything she now teaches.

The book is the marriage cornerstone of the Emotional Portfolio™ framework and The Covenant Economy™, the body of work behind EQGenix’s mission to Build Emotional Wealth™.

THE ECOSYSTEM

A cornerstone with a collection built around it.

The book anchors a growing set of resources for couples. A Companion Workbook turns the blueprint into week-by-week practice. The Sacred Union Set™ gathers Sacred Deposits™, Her Covenant Journal™, The Covenant Playbook™, and The Covenant Card™ in one keepsake-grade box, designed as a wedding and anniversary gift. Depósitos Sagrados, the Spanish-language edition, was written in Spanish rather than machine translated.

For congregations, the 5:1™ framework is also available as a premarital and marriage-formation curriculum that pastors and counselors can run year after year. Churches and counselors can start that conversation at the Sacred Deposits™ page.
