EQGenix Founder & CEO Yvonne G. Hall to Speak at the 2026 Veteran Ready Summit at West Point

EQGenix is proud to announce that Founder and Chief Executive Officer Yvonne G. Hall is scheduled to participate as a panel speaker at the 2026 Veteran Ready Summit, taking place December 13–15 at the United States Military Academy at West Point, New York.

The national summit brings together military leadership, employers, federal agencies, workforce development professionals, and veteran-serving organizations to advance practical strategies for veteran employment, career development, and long-term workforce retention.

“Our veterans have already demonstrated extraordinary discipline, resilience, and leadership in service to our nation. Our responsibility is to ensure those strengths are recognized, developed, and translated into meaningful opportunities beyond military service. At EQGenix, we believe readiness is not simply about securing the next position; it is about equipping individuals with the emotional and cognitive capacity to lead, adapt, and thrive in every environment.”

YVONNE G. HALL · FOUNDER & CEO, EQGENIX
PANEL “Registered Apprenticeship: The Pipeline You Can’t Hire Your Way Out Of” WHEN December 14, 2026 · 10:25–10:50 a.m. Eastern WHERE Eisenhower Hall, West Point, N.Y. PANELISTS Yvonne G. Hall, EQGenix
Bruce Townshend, Vetted Employers
Randy Wolken, MACNY, The Manufacturers Association
Benjamin Valinsky, International Union of Elevator Constructors
Dave Harrison, Fastport

The discussion will examine how organizations can develop structured apprenticeship pathways, translate military experience into civilian career opportunities, and leverage established workforce development programs.

Hall’s participation reflects EQGenix’s commitment to strengthening the systems that support veterans and transitioning service members.

Through initiatives such as Mission-Ready Civilian™, EQGenix focuses on developing emotional and cognitive capacity, translating military strengths into civilian environments, and supporting sustainable professional transitions. Participation through Veterans Readiness and Employment (VR&E) is subject to individual authorization by a veteran’s VR&E counselor and does not constitute blanket VA program approval or endorsement.

This speaking engagement represents an opportunity to contribute EQGenix’s perspective to national conversations surrounding veteran readiness, workforce development, and institutional leadership.

WHY THIS CONVERSATION

Hiring is not the bottleneck. Readiness and retention are.

The panel’s title makes a blunt argument: organizations cannot simply hire their way out of a workforce gap. Registered apprenticeship offers a structured alternative, with defined pathways, on-the-job learning, and a clear line from military experience to a civilian career.

For veterans, the hardest part of that line is often translation. Military experience is real, demonstrated capability, but it is rarely stated in terms a civilian employer recognizes, and the operating environment around it changes overnight.

HALL’S PERSPECTIVE

Human capacity formation in the age of AI.

Hall brings EQGenix’s focus on human capacity formation to the discussion: the sound judgment, regulation, and readiness that determine whether a worker thrives inside a new system, not just whether they are placed in it. As civilian work increasingly runs through AI tools, her emphasis is on human agency, meaning the person decides, verifies, and can demonstrate the work without the tool.

She is a NAMA CAMS-II certified Applied Behavioral Researcher and a former Guardian ad Litem, and she leads EQGenix as Founder and Chief Executive Officer.

Her connection to veteran service is also personal. Hall comes from a military family, and her grandfather was a Tuskegee Airman. Seeing loved ones come home from service changed is part of what drove her to build Mission-Ready Civilian™ and to focus on helping veterans carry their strengths into civilian life.

MISSION-READY CIVILIAN™

The gap is not competence. It is translation.

Mission-Ready Civilian™ is a twelve-week, twelve-module employment-readiness program for veterans, delivered by EQGenix Institute LLC. It trains five capacities with direct employment functions: identity stability, emotional regulation, relational command, functional translation, and purpose and legacy.

The program carries an AI spine through every module built on three questions: who decides, what gets checked, and what can the veteran demonstrate. It is designed to restate military capability in employer-legible terms and to close with the veteran performing the work alone.

Mission-Ready Civilian™ is training and skills development, not therapy or clinical care. Authorization is issued per veteran by a Vocational Rehabilitation Counselor under an Individual Written Rehabilitation Plan.

THE SUMMIT

Where military leadership meets the people who hire.

Held at the United States Military Academy at West Point, the Veteran Ready Summit convenes military leadership, employers, federal agencies, workforce development professionals, and veteran-serving organizations. Sessions take place at Eisenhower Hall, and Hall’s panel is part of the Federal Programs track. The full schedule is on the summit agenda.

The Veteran Ready Summit is organized by Fastport and held at West Point. The venue does not imply endorsement of EQGenix by the United States Military Academy or West Point.
