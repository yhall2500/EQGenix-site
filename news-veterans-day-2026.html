Honoring Those Who Served: EQGenix Recognizes Veterans Day 2026

Today, we honor the courage, sacrifice, and enduring commitment of the men and women who have worn our nation’s uniform.

At EQGenix, Veterans Day is more than a moment of recognition. It is a reminder that service does not end when the uniform comes off, and neither should our nation’s commitment to those who have served.

Our veterans bring extraordinary strengths to their families, workplaces, and communities—discipline, adaptability, integrity, resilience, and leadership forged through experiences few civilians will ever fully understand.

Yet the transition from military service to civilian life represents more than a change in profession. It is a new chapter that deserves meaningful opportunity, strong support systems, and environments where those strengths are recognized and valued.

Through our Mission-Ready Civilian™ initiative, EQGenix is committed to advancing the emotional and cognitive capacity that supports veterans as they navigate new roles, responsibilities, and opportunities beyond military service.

We also recognize the military spouses, children, and families whose sacrifices and unwavering support are an essential part of our nation’s story of service.

To every veteran: Your service matters. Your experience carries value. And your next chapter deserves the same dignity, purpose, and opportunity that defined your commitment to this nation.

From all of us at EQGenix, thank you for your service.
