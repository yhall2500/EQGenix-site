Government & Institutional Procurement

Everything an agency, district, or institutional buyer needs to evaluate, scope, and contract with EQGenix — on one page.

1 · CORPORATE IDENTITY

EQGenix Inc., a California corporation. 260 Newport Center Drive, Suite 100, Newport Beach, CA 92660. Programs are delivered under the EQGenix practice architecture, including Sovereign Shield™ (law enforcement), Sovereign Station Standard™ (fire service), Mission-Ready Civilian™ (veteran transition), and Grit to Genius™ (youth formation).

2 · REGISTRATIONS & CREDENTIALS

EQGenix Inc. is a California POST presenter (Presenter ID D13-U49). Entity registration details — including UEI and SAM.gov status — are provided with every quote and are available to contracting officers on request. Mission-Ready Civilian program specifications for VR&E authorization are documented in the official Provider Packet.

3 · DOCUMENTATION AVAILABLE ON REQUEST

W-9 · Certificate of Insurance (COI) · Capability statement · Program specifications and rate schedules · Sample agreements. Documentation is issued directly to the requesting agency’s procurement or risk-management contact — email contact@eqgenix.com with the document you need and the agency it is for.

4 · QUOTES & RFP RESPONSES

Request a quote or submit an RFP through the inquiry form or directly to contact@eqgenix.com · 888-824-7244. Quotes are written against a defined scope and hold for the period stated on their face.

5 · SCOPE-DEVELOPMENT PROCESS

Engagements follow a fixed sequence: (1) a command or leadership briefing; (2) a written scope — population, delivery format, measurement plan, and schedule; (3) a written proposal for command, council, or board review; (4) executed agreement or authorization. No engagement begins, and no obligation is created, before a signed agreement.

6 · DIFFERENTIATION, STATED FACTUALLY

EQGenix builds formation infrastructure, not awareness training: named curricula with sequential delivery, a measured entry baseline and post-program differential for every participant, and written artifacts an agency retains. Whether these facts support a particular procurement pathway is a determination each agency makes under its own procurement rules.

7 · CONTRACTING CONTACT

All procurement correspondence: contact@eqgenix.com · 888-824-7244. Reference the program and agency in the subject line and the request routes to the right desk the same day.
