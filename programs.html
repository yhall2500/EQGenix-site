How do individuals and institutions get started?＋

Individuals and families can open a program page to review its format and next steps. Schools, departments, employers, and churches can start a partnership inquiry to discuss fit and delivery.

Where can I find pricing and availability?＋

Each program page provides its own enrollment, purchase, or inquiry route. For an organizational proposal, contact the partnership team.

What evidence supports the frameworks?＋

Our research library and evidence page describe the theoretical foundations and evidence status. Publication of a framework is distinct from empirical validation of a program’s effectiveness.

Is Christian grounding part of the work?＋

EQGenix’s stewardship-centered philosophy has explicit Christian grounding. Some offerings, including Grit to Genius™ Homeschool and Sacred Deposits™, expressly incorporate Scripture or Christian teaching. Review the specific program for its content and fit.
