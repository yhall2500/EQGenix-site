02 INSTITUTIONAL ANALYSIS Emotional Wealth as Universal Currency: A New Framework for Human Capital Valuation Traditional human capital metrics measure what people produce. The Emotional Portfolio™ measures what they can endure, create, and sustain. This paper argues for a fundamental revaluation. DOWNLOAD THE PAPER (PDF)
03 EVIDENCE REVIEW Trained for the Call, Unbuilt for the Career: The Evidence Gap in First Responder Wellness, and the Case for Formation Why the field has concentrated almost all of its effort after the incident, what the systematic reviews actually say about the interventions agencies deploy most, and what a defensible pre-incident standard would have to satisfy. 04 APPLIED RESEARCH What Help Leaves Behind: Relief, Service, and the Case for Capacity Formation The testable dependency claim is not supported by the randomized cash-transfer evidence. This paper sets that claim aside, relocates the argument to a distinction that can actually be measured — relief versus formation — and states plainly which side has the weakest evidence base: its own. DOWNLOAD THE PAPER (PDF)
05 FIELD REPORT From Barracks to Boardroom: Translating Military Cognitive Discipline Into Civilian Capital Written for Veteran Readiness and Employment counselors and anyone deciding whether a formation program is worth a Chapter 31 authorization. The transition challenge is not only résumé translation but the ability to name, evidence, and exercise military-acquired capacity in civilian settings — and a provider’s first obligation is to produce evidence rather than to claim it. DOWNLOAD THE PAPER (PDF)
06 POSITION PAPER The Formation Gap: What Is Lost Between Evidence and Delivery — The Youth Application The common claim that character education does not work is not supported by the evidence — the meta-analytic record is positive and large. The problem lies in a six-link chain from design through durability, of which the last four links are measured inconsistently and rarely linked to outcomes. This paper defines that gap and states what would refute the framework proposed. DOWNLOAD THE PAPER (PDF)
07 STRATEGIC ANALYSIS The AI Displacement Paradox: Why Automation Makes Human Capacity More Valuable, Not Less The conventional narrative frames AI as a threat to human relevance. The evidence suggests the opposite — that as technical skills are automated, the economic premium on distinctly human capacities accelerates. DOWNLOAD THE PAPER (PDF)
