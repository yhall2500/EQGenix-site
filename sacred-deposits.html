THE CORNERSTONE

A Blueprint, Not a Devotional.
A Practice, Not a Feeling.

Most marriages don’t fail. They erode.

The distance grows gradually — through patterns repeated so often they become invisible. Sacred Deposits™ names those patterns and gives a couple the structure to interrupt them before they become the story.

Built on the 5:1™ framework.

Five deposits for every withdrawal — translated into a daily operating model grounded in behavioral science and covenant commitment. What a couple walks away with is language, structure, and a discipline they can keep.

For couples who want to protect, not just repair.

Most marriage resources work in crisis. This book works upstream — for the couple that is still intact, still functioning, and privately aware that something needs strengthening before it breaks.
