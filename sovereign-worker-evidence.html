THE FOUR QUESTIONS DILIGENCE ASKS

Every Answer, On the Record.

01WHAT IS MEASURED?

Capacity across the five pillars of the Emotional Portfolio™, at entry and exit of every engagement. Measurements of capacity — not diagnoses, not clinical instruments, not evaluations of any condition. Full constructs on The Five.

02WHAT IS NOT CLAIMED?

No numeric cutoff is published as a decision boundary — none has completed psychometric validation. No instrument assigns a classification or deficit label to an individual. A number without provenance does not appear on this page.

03HOW IS IT GOVERNED?

Five non-waivable rules, published identically on the Assessment Use Policy and in the master services agreement: no individual profile reaches an employer, no score informs a personnel decision, aggregate-only reporting with a stated minimum cell size.

04WHAT IS IT BUILT ON?

The five-pillar construct spine, documented in the EQGenix working-paper series with live citations and a four-class evidence taxonomy — read the papers. Entry-to-exit measurement is documented in the methodology.

THE PROVENANCE RULE

Every number that publishes here carries its source, publisher, year, and retrieval date — and is re-verified before each external use.

Validated outcome data publishes as engagements complete. Until then, this page holds the architecture — not placeholders.
